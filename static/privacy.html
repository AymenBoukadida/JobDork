Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

JobScout Dorks is a free tool that generates Google search queries for job hunting. We designed it to collect as little data as possible. This policy explains what happens to information you provide when using the app.

What we collect

  • Search parameters: job titles, skills, location, and similar fields you enter to build queries. These are sent to our server only when you click Generate dorks or upload a CV for parsing.
  • CV uploads: if you use the CV parser, your file is processed in memory on the server to extract text. Files are not stored on disk or in a database after the request completes.
  • Browser storage: recent searches are saved in your browser's localStorage on your device. We do not receive this history unless you share it with us manually.

What we do not collect

  • No user accounts or login credentials
  • No payment information
  • No analytics cookies or third-party advertising trackers in the default app
  • No sale of personal data

How your data is used

Search parameters are used solely to generate Google dork query strings and return them to your browser. CV text extraction is used solely to pre-fill the search form. We do not use your inputs to train models or build marketing profiles.

Third-party services

When you click Open in Google, you leave this app and are subject to Google's privacy policy. We load fonts from Google Fonts; your browser may request them from Google's servers.

Data retention

Server-side request data is not persisted beyond normal web-server logs (if any), which your host may retain according to its own policy. Search history in localStorage remains on your device until you clear it via the app or your browser settings.

Your choices

  • Clear recent searches using the Clear all button in the History panel
  • Clear site data in your browser to remove all locally stored history
  • Use manual entry instead of CV upload if you prefer not to send a resume file to the server

Contact

If you self-host this project, you are the data controller for your instance. For the public demo, contact the site operator listed on the deployment you are using.
